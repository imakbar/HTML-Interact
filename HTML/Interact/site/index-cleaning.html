FAQ

Good to know

Do I need to be home during the clean?
Not at all. Many customers give us a key or access code. You can also stay, whichever you prefer.
Do you bring your own supplies?
Yes. We bring all equipment and eco-friendly products. If you prefer particular products, just let us know.
What if I am not happy with the result?
Tell us within 24 hours and we will return to re-clean the areas of concern at no charge.
Are your cleaners insured and background-checked?
Every cleaner is vetted, trained by us and covered by our liability insurance.
How do I reschedule or cancel?
Free rescheduling or cancellation up to 24 hours before your appointment via phone or email.
