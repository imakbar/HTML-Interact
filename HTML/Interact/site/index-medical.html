FAQ

Questions patients ask us

Do I need an appointment?
Walk-ins are welcome for urgent care. For specialist consultations we recommend booking online so you are seen with minimal waiting.
Which insurance plans do you accept?
We work with most major insurers. Bring your card to your visit and our front desk will confirm your coverage in advance.
How quickly will I get my test results?
Most blood tests and screenings are ready the same day. Imaging reports are normally available within 24 hours via our patient portal.
Is parking available?
Yes, free patient parking is available behind the main building, with step-free access to reception.
Can I get a second opinion?
Absolutely. Our specialists regularly review outside records and can arrange a second-opinion consultation.
