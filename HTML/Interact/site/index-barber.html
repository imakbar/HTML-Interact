Our story

Barbering the traditional way

Iron & Oak opened in 2012 with one chair and a simple idea: treat every customer like family and every haircut like a craft. Today we are a neighbourhood institution, where the banter is good and the fades are better.

  • Master barbers with 10+ years experience
  • Hot towels, premium oils and tonics
  • Cold drinks and a relaxed lounge
